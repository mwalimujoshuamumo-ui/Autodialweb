AutoDial – Agent Automation App

AutoDial is an Android automation application designed for agents to manage and process data offers and customer transactions more efficiently.

  • 📱 Automatic M-Pesa SMS detection and transaction processing
  • ⚡ Automatic offer matching based on payment amount
  • 📞 USSD automation for supported offers
  • 🔄 Processes pending and unprocessed M-Pesa messages
  • 📊 Recent transactions with Completed, Pending, Failed and Skipped statuses
  • 💰 Airtime and commission management
  • 📅 Offer scheduling and automation controls
  • 👥 Customer and contact management
  • 🔐 Secure settings and automation controls
  • 📡 Support for configured SIM/USSD operations

  3. Open the downloaded APK from your Downloads folder.

  5. Install AutoDial.
